### sv - partly right (0.67 / 2)

> Which of these increase stroke volume?

**Your answer:** Greater venous return (preload); Stronger myocardial contractility; Vagal stimulation of the SA node

**Correct answer:** Greater venous return (preload); Stronger myocardial contractility; Lower arterial resistance (afterload)

**Explanation:**

More filling stretches the muscle and it ejects more (Frank-Starling), more contractility ejects more, and lower afterload is easier to eject against. Higher afterload lowers stroke volume, and vagal tone mainly slows the rate rather than changing stroke volume directly.

### frank-starling (4 points) - 45 words

> Explain the Frank-Starling mechanism and why it matters.

**Answer:**

```
Greater filling of the ventricle before contraction stretches the muscle fibres, so the force of contraction rises and the stroke volume is larger for the same heart rate. This is why the left and right sides of the heart stay balanced when venous return changes.
```
