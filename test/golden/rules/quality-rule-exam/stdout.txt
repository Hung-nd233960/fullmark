explanation.short  (quality rule)
level: error  (profile exam, under profile exam)
options:
  min-words = 8  (profile exam)

What it catches: an explanation is too short to teach anything
Why it matters: An explanation should say why the answer is right and what the wrong ones get wrong.

An example that triggers it:
    ---
    title: Example
    ---
    
    ## Pick one. {type=single id=q}
    
    - [x] a
    - [ ] b
    
    ::: explanation
    Because.
    :::

How to fix it: say why the answer is right and what the wrong ones get wrong
