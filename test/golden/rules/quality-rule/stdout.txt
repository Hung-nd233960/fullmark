choice.correct-longest  (quality rule)
level: warning  (built-in, under profile default)
options:
  min-length = 40  (built-in)
  ratio = 1.8  (built-in)

What it catches: the right choice is much longer than every wrong one
Why it matters: A learner can pick the longest choice without knowing the answer.

An example that triggers it:
    ---
    title: Example
    ---
    
    ## Why is energy conserved? {type=single id=q}
    
    - [x] Because the total energy of a closed and isolated system stays constant
    - [ ] Luck
    - [ ] Chance

How to fix it: shorten the right choice, or lengthen the wrong ones to match
