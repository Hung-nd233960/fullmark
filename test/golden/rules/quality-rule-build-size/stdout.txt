build.size  (quality rule)
level: warning  (built-in, under profile default)
options:
  warn-mb = 20  (built-in)
  max-mb = 100  (built-in)

What it catches: the built page is large (checked by `build`, from step 2 of the order of work)
Why it matters: A page embeds its images, fonts and maths, and a very large file is slow to open and to send. `quiz build` measures the page, which `check` cannot: this rule only holds the level and the limits, so that profiles can set them before `build` exists.

An example that triggers it:
    (checked by `quiz build`: a quiz whose page is over 20 MB, with warn-mb at its default)

How to fix it: shrink or remove large images; at max-mb (100 by default) the build refuses regardless of the level
