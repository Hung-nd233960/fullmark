What is the Fourier transform of a rectangular pulse?

{{c.letter}} {{c.text}} {{L.correct}}
{{L.explanation}}
A rectangular pulse of width T has the spectrum T·sinc(fT): a window in time is a sinc in frequency. The narrower the pulse, the wider the spectrum.

Fourier transform properties

Property x(t) X(ω)
Linearity a·x(t) + b·y(t) a·X(ω) + b·Y(ω)
Time shift x(t − t₀) e−jωt₀·X(ω)
Scaling x(at) (1/|a|)·X(ω/a)
Convolution x(t) ∗ y(t) X(ω)·Y(ω)

sinc(x) = sin(πx) / (πx), so sinc(0) = 1.

Trigonometric identities

  • ejθ = cos θ + j·sin θ
  • cos θ = (ejθ + e−jθ) / 2
  • sin² θ = (1 − cos 2θ) / 2
