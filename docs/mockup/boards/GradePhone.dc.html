Derive the transfer function of an RC low-pass filter with the Laplace transform, and say what its pole tells you.

Answer · 71 words

With zero initial conditions, I(s) = sC·Vout(s), and the loop gives Vin(s) = (sRC + 1)·Vout(s). So H(s) = 1 / (1 + sRC). The pole is at s = −1/RC and sets the cut-off frequency.
